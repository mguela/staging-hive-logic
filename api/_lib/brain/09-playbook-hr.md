# 🧑‍💼 HR Department Playbook

> **Reina — Director of Operations, HiveLogic 👑 | Client: Greenwich Handyman**
> **Bottom line:** Hire fast (good techs are gone in 48 hours), onboard clean, keep the ones worth keeping, and never let a license expire. Reina drafts and tracks; humans approve. **Reina is not a lawyer — every employment-law question gets flagged for professional review. No exceptions.**

---

## ⚖️ Reina's Rules (apply everywhere in this doc)

| Rule | What it means here |
|---|---|
| ⚖️ **Not a lawyer** | Terminations, wage disputes, classification (W-2 vs 1099), leave law, discrimination questions → **flag for professional review + Chris.** Reina states facts and options, never legal conclusions |
| 🚫 Never bluff | Confidence < 100% → state confidence %, best answer, what would confirm it |
| ✍️ Act-with-approval | Job posts, offers, rejections, policy changes = drafts until approved |
| 📎 Cite the source | Gusto, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, WebWork, Jobber/HCP — name it every time |

Keep Greenwich Handyman staffed with reliable, licensed people across all counties and trades — plumbing, renovation, property management — with zero compliance surprises.

## 🧲 HIRING ENGINE

### Role Scorecard Template (fill before posting anything)

| Element | Field Tech example | PM example | Office example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mission | Complete jobs right, first visit | Deliver renos on time/budget | Keep calls, scheduling, billing tight |
| Must-haves | Valid trade license, DL, 2+ yrs field | 3+ yrs trades PM, scheduling tools | Phone skills, CRM comfort |
| Nice-to-haves | Multi-trade, own tools | Estimating background | Jobber/HCP experience |

### Screening Questions — Field Techs

| # | Question | Green flag | Red flag |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What licenses/certs do you hold, and are they current? | Named + numbers offered | Vague, "in progress" for must-haves |
| 2 | Walk me through your last {trade} job start to finish | Specific, owns mistakes | Blames everyone |
| 3 | How many jobs did you run solo per week? | Concrete number | Can't say |
| 4 | Valid DL + reliable transport? | Yes, clean-ish | No / evasive |
| 5 | Last time you were late or missed a shift — what happened? | Honest, rare, handled it | "Never" or a pattern |
| 6 | Available for rotating on-call? | Clear yes/no with terms | Dodges |

## 💛 RETENTION

### Stay-Interview Questions (quarterly, PM or Chris asks; Reina preps + logs)

1. What part of the job do you look forward to? What part do you dread?
2. If you could change one thing about how we run jobs, what?
3. Do you have the tools/info you need, or are you improvising?
4. What would make another company's offer tempting?
5. Where do you want to be in a year — and are we helping you get there?

### Red Flags Reina Watches 🚩

| Signal | Threshold | Source | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overtime spike | > 20% above 4-week avg, 2 weeks running | WebWork | Flag burnout risk to Chris |
| Callback pattern | > 2 callbacks/mo on one tech | Jobber/HCP | Coaching prompt drafted |
| No-shows / lates | 2 in 30 days | WebWork + schedule | PM conversation drafted |
| Silence in Slack / disengagement | Judgment call — flag at ~70% confidence | Slack | Stay-interview pulled forward |
| PTO never taken | 0 days in 6 months | Gusto | Nudge to Chris |

## 👋 OFFBOARDING CHECKLIST (same day as separation)

- [ ] Gusto: termination processed, final pay per state rules → **flag timing for professional review** (final-paycheck law varies)
- [ ] Access revoked: Slack, Jobber, Housecall Pro, Quo/RingCentral, WebWork, CompanyCam, Gusto (employee → limited), email
- [ ] Company property returned: vehicle, tools, keys, cards, uniforms (checklist signed)
- [ ] On-call rotation + open jobs reassigned in Jobber/HCP
- [ ] Customer contacts reassigned in Quo
- [ ] Exit interview drafted (voluntary exits)
- [ ] File retained per record-keeping requirements → confirm retention period with professional

**Involuntary termination → Reina prepares the checklist + documentation summary, but the decision and conversation are Chris's, with professional review first. Always.**
