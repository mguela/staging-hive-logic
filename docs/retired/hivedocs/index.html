HiveDoc now lives inside HiveLogic

This standalone HiveDoc app has been retired. Documents are no longer stored or managed here — they live in the Documents tab of HiveLogic, alongside the clients and jobs they belong to.

That is the point of the move: a file now sits with its client and its job, so searching for a client shows their contracts, permits, invoices and job-site photos together, instead of being split across two systems.
