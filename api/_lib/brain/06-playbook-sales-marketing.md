Capture on **every** lead, no exceptions:

| Field | Why | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Source (referral / 99Calls / ads / GBP / Craigslist / PM contract) | ROI tracking | Jobber/HCP custom field |

## 🌡️ Lead Scoring

| Tier | Criteria (any) | Reina's action |
|---|---|---|
| 🔥 **HOT** | Emergency; stated budget; repeat customer; PM contract work; asked for a date | Call task NOW + text draft; owner pinged if untouched 15 min |
| 🌤️ **WARM** | This-month timeline; referral without urgency; quote requested, no date | Contact same day; full follow-up cadence |
| 🧊 **COLD** | "Just pricing"; no timeline; out of service area edge | Auto-drip cadence only; no tech time until they engage |

**Re-score on every touch.** A cold lead that replies twice becomes warm automatically.

## 📊 Pipeline Stages + Stall Alarms

| Stage | Definition | Max days before Reina flags 🚨 |
|---|---|---|
| New | Lead captured, no contact yet | **1 day** (same day for HOT) |
| Contacted | Two-way contact made | **3 days** without next step booked |
| Quoted | Quote sent (Jobber/HCP) | **7 days** without decision |
| Follow-up | Active cadence running | **14 days** → move to Won/Lost decision |

**Lost reason codes (required):** price / timing / went-with-competitor / ghosted / out-of-scope.
Source: Jobber + Housecall Pro pipeline reports.

## 🔁 Follow-Up Cadence (quote sent = Day 0)

All drafts, all approved before sending via Quo.

**Day 1 — text:**
> Hi {Name}, it's {Rep} with Greenwich Handyman. Wanted to make sure the quote for your {job} came through okay. Any questions I can answer? We can usually get you on the schedule this week.

**Day 3 — text:**
> Hi {Name}, following up on the {job} quote ({$amount}). Happy to walk through options or adjust scope if the number isn't sitting right. What works for you?

**Day 7 — email:**
> Subject: Your {job} quote — still good to go
> Hi {Name}, your quote is still valid and we have openings the week of {date}. If timing or budget is the holdup, tell me — we'd rather adjust than lose you. Reply or call {number}.

**Day 14 — text (breakup):**
> Hi {Name}, last check-in on the {job} quote — I don't want to pester you. If now's not the time, no problem; I'll keep your quote on file. If you'd like it scheduled, one word and it's done.

**No response after Day 14 → Lost (ghosted) → win-back queue.**

## 🔄 Win-Back Campaign (Lost Quotes, Day 90)

| Trigger | Draft |
|---|---|

Skip anyone who said "do not contact." Source: Jobber/HCP Lost list.

## 🙌 Referral Ask SOP

**Trigger: job marked complete in Jobber/HCP + no complaints on file (Quo threads clean).**

| Step | Timing | Draft |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Review request | Same day | See template below |
