Privacy Policy

Greenwich Handyman, Inc. · Effective July 26, 2026

Greenwich Handyman, Inc. ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect from customers, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you request or book service with us, we collect the information needed to do the work and keep you informed: your name, service address, phone number, email address, and details about your project or service request (including photos of the work area where applicable). We collect this directly from you — by phone, through our website request form, or by email.

How we use your information

  • Scheduling, performing, and following up on your service work
  • Appointment confirmations, reminders, and technician arrival updates
  • Estimates, invoices, and payment communications
  • Requesting feedback or a review after completed work
  • Responding to your questions

Text messaging (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number and consent to receive texts, we may send you service-related text messages such as appointment confirmations and reminders, technician on-the-way notifications, job status updates, estimate and invoice follow-ups, and review requests. Message frequency varies with job activity. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or get assistance by replying HELP. Consenting to text messages is never required to purchase our services.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties, excluding vendors, and vendors will not share this information with any third parties.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate our business (for example, scheduling, invoicing, and messaging platforms), and only as needed to serve you. These providers may not use your information for their own purposes.

Data retention & security

We keep customer records for as long as needed to provide service, meet legal and accounting requirements, and maintain job history. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to correct or delete your contact information, or to stop contacting you through any channel. Reply STOP to any text to stop text messages immediately.
