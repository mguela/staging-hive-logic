HiveLogic Field

Privacy Policy · Greenwich Handyman, Inc. (GH Group)

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Overview

HiveLogic Field is a workforce management app used by employees and contractors of Greenwich Handyman, Inc. ("GH Group," "we," "us") to clock in and out, view job assignments, navigate to job sites, and submit photos and reports for completed work. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and how it's used.

This app is provided for internal business use by GH Group employees and authorized subcontractors. It is not available to the general public.

Information We Collect

  • Account & login information. Your name, email address, and employee/contractor role, used to authenticate you and control access to job data.
  • Location data. While you are clocked in, the app collects GPS location to verify job-site arrival/departure, support route navigation, and confirm accurate time tracking. Location is not collected while you are clocked out or when the app is closed.
  • Camera & photos. Photos you take or upload through the app for job documentation, before/after records, and completion reports.
  • Push notification tokens. A device identifier used to deliver job assignments, schedule changes, and reminders to your device.
  • Usage & device information. Basic technical data (device type, app version, crash logs) used to keep the app reliable.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate core app features: clock in/out, job routing, scheduling, and job reporting.
  • To verify time and attendance for payroll purposes.
  • To send you job-related push notifications.
  • To maintain accurate business and safety records for completed work.
  • To troubleshoot issues and improve app reliability.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected through this app is used internally by GH Group for business operations (payroll, scheduling, job costing) and is shared only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the app (cloud hosting, authentication, and notification delivery), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Your employer/supervisor within GH Group, as part of normal job and time-tracking oversight.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

Data Retention

We retain job, time-tracking, and photo records for as long as needed for business, payroll, and legal record-keeping purposes. You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us below, subject to any records we're legally required to keep.

Your Choices

  • Location access can be controlled through your device's app permissions. Disabling it will limit clock-in verification and routing features.
  • Camera access can be controlled through your device's app permissions. Disabling it will prevent photo uploads.
  • Push notifications can be turned off in your device settings.

Security

We use industry-standard measures, including encrypted connections and access-controlled cloud infrastructure, to protect the information collected through this app.

Children's Privacy

HiveLogic Field is intended for use by employees and contractors and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
