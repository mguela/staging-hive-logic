Privacy Policy

HiveLogic · Effective August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HiveLogic, developed and operated by Greenwich Handyman, Inc. ("we," "us," "our"), collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the HiveLogic application and website (the "Service").

Who uses HiveLogic

HiveLogic is internal business software used by Greenwich Handyman, Inc. to run its own construction/home-service operations. It is not offered as a public consumer app, and it does not collect information from the general public through the Service itself.

Information we collect

  • Business operational data: job, scheduling, estimating, invoicing, and financial records entered by our team.
  • Client and lead information: contact details and service history for people and businesses we work with.
  • Employee/contractor information: the limited data needed for scheduling, dispatch, and payroll-adjacent workflows.
  • Marketing content and connected-platform data: drafts, media, and performance metrics for content we publish through connected accounts such as Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok.

How we use TikTok data specifically

Where HiveLogic is connected to a TikTok account via TikTok's Content Posting API, we use that connection solely to publish or schedule video content to our own authorized TikTok account on behalf of Greenwich Handyman, Inc., and to retrieve basic publishing status and performance metrics for that content. We do not use TikTok's API to access, collect, or store data belonging to other TikTok users or accounts, and we do not sell or share TikTok data with third parties for advertising.

How we use information

We use the information above to operate the business: schedule and cost jobs, communicate with clients, run payroll-adjacent workflows, and plan and publish marketing content. AI features within the Service may process this data to draft content or surface recommendations; a person reviews and approves any action with a real-world or financial effect.

How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with the service providers necessary to run HiveLogic (for example, our cloud hosting and database providers, and connected platforms you've explicitly authorized, such as TikTok or Meta, for the specific purpose of publishing content), or when required by law.

Data retention and security

We retain business records for as long as needed for operational and legal purposes, and we use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data, including access controls and encrypted connections.

Your rights

If you are a client, lead, or contractor whose information we hold and you'd like to access, correct, or request deletion of it, contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.

Children's privacy

The Service is business software and is not directed to children; we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the effective date above reflects the latest revision.
